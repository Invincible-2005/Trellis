# Travel and Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance Manager · **Applies to:** everyone who travels or spends money for Kestrel · **Effective:** 1 April 2026

This policy tells you how to plan a work trip, what you may spend, and how to claim it back. Approvers follow the separate finance approval procedures; this document covers what travellers need to know.

## 1. Before you travel

- **Every work trip needs your manager's approval before you book anything.** Raise a travel request in the Kestrel Expense portal with the purpose, dates and estimated cost.
- Trips with an estimated total cost **above ₹40,000** also need the **Finance Manager's** approval.
- **International trips** need the **Admin's** approval as well, at least **21 days** before departure.
- Book through the company travel desk (travel@kestrelsoftware.example) whenever possible. The travel desk pays directly, so you don't need to claim those costs.

## 2. Getting there

| Distance or situation | What you may book |
|---|---|
| Train journey up to 8 hours | Train, AC 2-tier or AC chair car |
| Train journey longer than 8 hours | Flight, **economy class only** |
| Any trip with a client meeting the same morning | Flight allowed even if the train is shorter, with manager approval |
| Within a city | Cab or auto, up to **₹1,200 per day** |

Business class is never reimbursed, including on international flights. Personal upgrades with your own points or money are fine.

## 3. Staying there

Hotel limits are **per night, including taxes**:

| City category | Cities | Hotel limit per night | Daily allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metro | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune | ₹5,000 | ₹1,500 |
| Other cities | Everywhere else in India | ₹3,000 | ₹1,000 |
| International | Any country | Pre-approved per trip | USD 60 |

- The **daily allowance** covers meals and small expenses. You don't need receipts for it, and you can't claim meals separately on the same day.
- If you stay with friends or family, you can't claim a hotel, but you still get the daily allowance.
- Hotel bills above the limit are reimbursed only up to the limit unless the Finance Manager approved the exception before the trip.

## 4. Other expenses

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Client dinner | Up to ₹2,500 per person, names of attendees required |
| Conference fees | Need Engineering Manager approval before registering |
| Laundry | Only for trips longer than 4 nights |
| Alcohol | Never reimbursed |
| Fines (traffic, late check-out) | Never reimbursed |

## 5. Claiming your money back

1. Submit your claim in the Kestrel Expense portal **within 15 days** of returning.
2. Attach a receipt for **every item above ₹500**. Photos of paper receipts are fine if they are readable.
3. Your manager approves the claim, then Finance checks it.
4. Claims approved by the **20th of a month** are paid with **that month's salary**. Claims approved later are paid the next month.

**Late claims:** claims submitted after 15 days need the Finance Manager's approval. Claims submitted **more than 60 days** after the trip are rejected.

## 6. Company cards

A small number of people (managers who travel often) have a company credit card. Personal spending on a company card is not allowed, even if you pay it back. Card holders submit their statement with receipts by the **5th of each month**.

## 7. Misuse

Claiming expenses that weren't for work, splitting one bill into smaller claims to avoid receipts, or submitting the same receipt twice is treated as a conduct violation and is reported to the HR Manager.
