# HR Operations: Recruitment, Onboarding and Exits

**Owner:** HR Manager · **For:** the HR team (HR Executives and above) · **Version:** 3.1, 1 June 2026

This is the working guide for the HR Executives who run hiring, joining and exits day to day. Salary bands and increments are in the separate compensation document, which only the HR Manager holds.

## 1. Hiring process

| Step | Owner | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Job description approved | Engineering Manager + HR | Before posting |
| 2. Recruiter screen (30 min call) | HR Executive | Within 3 working days of application |
| 3. Technical round | Engineering | Scheduled within 5 working days of screen |
| 4. System design round (E3 and above) | Engineering | |
| 5. Values round | Hiring manager | |
| 6. Hire/no-hire decision | Hiring manager | Within 2 working days of last round |
| 7. Offer letter | HR Executive | Within 2 working days of the decision |

- Candidates who are rejected get a polite email within **5 working days** of the decision. Never leave a candidate without a reply.
- Interview feedback must be filled in the HR portal within **24 hours** of the interview.

## 2. Offers

- Offer letters are generated from the HR portal template. The salary figure comes from the HR Manager; HR Executives don't decide it.
- An offer is **valid for 7 calendar days**. Extensions need the HR Manager's approval.
- Joining date should be within **60 days** of acceptance; longer gaps need the hiring manager's agreement.

## 3. Background verification

- Started as soon as the offer is accepted, through our verification vendor.
- Covers education, the last two employers, identity and address.
- The vendor's report is due within **10 working days**. Joining can go ahead while it's pending, but any red flag must be escalated to the HR Manager the same day.

## 4. Before day 1 (joining checklist)

The HR Executive makes sure that, **3 working days before joining**:

- [ ] Laptop request is raised with IT (needs 3 working days).
- [ ] Trellis and Slack accounts are created and the person is added to the right team.
- [ ] Desk is booked for the first week (5 days, so the new joiner doesn't need the desk app on day 1).
- [ ] A buddy from the team is assigned.
- [ ] The welcome email with the day-1 schedule is sent.

**Documents the joiner must bring:** PAN card, Aadhaar, degree certificates, relieving letter from the last employer, last 3 payslips, a cancelled cheque for salary.

## 5. Day 1 and the first month

- Day 1 starts at **10:00** at the Bengaluru front desk (09:30 in Pune).
- Induction session at 11:00: values, Code of Conduct, information security, tools.
- Security training must be completed **within the first 7 days**.
- HR checks in with every new joiner at the end of **week 2 and week 4**.

## 6. Probation

- Full-time employees have a **6-month probation**. Interns don't have probation.
- Notice period during probation is **15 days** on both sides.
- The HR Executive sends the probation review form to the manager **2 weeks before** the probation ends. Confirmation letters go out within 7 days after.

## 7. Notice periods (after probation)

| Level | Notice period |
|---|---|
| E1, E2 | 30 days |
| E3, E4 | 60 days |
| E5, Engineering Managers and above | 90 days |

Notice can be shortened by the HR Manager when the handover is complete. Leave can't be used to shorten notice unless the manager agrees in writing.

## 8. Referral bonus

| Hired level | Bonus to the referrer |
|---|---|
| E1, E2 | ₹25,000 |
| E3 and above, managers | ₹50,000 |

The bonus is paid after the new hire completes **90 days**. HR and the hiring manager for the role can't earn a referral bonus for it.

## 9. Exits

- Exit interview by an HR Executive in the last week.
- IT disables accounts at **18:00 on the last working day**; equipment is collected the same day.
- The **full and final settlement** is paid within **45 days** of the last working day. Experience and relieving letters are issued with it.
