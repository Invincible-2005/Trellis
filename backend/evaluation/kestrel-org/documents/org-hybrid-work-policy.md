# Hybrid Work Policy

**Owner:** HR Manager · **Applies to:** all full-time employees · **Effective:** 1 July 2026

Kestrel works in a hybrid model: we come to the office for collaboration and work from home for focused work. This policy explains how many days you're expected in the office, how to book a desk, and what support you get for working from home. Interns follow the office rules in the Intern Program Guide for their first month.

## 1. Office locations

| Office | Address | Access hours |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru (head office) | 4th floor, Sector 6, HSR Layout | 08:00 to 22:00, Monday to Saturday |
| Pune | 2nd floor, Baner Road | 09:00 to 21:00, Monday to Friday |

Outside access hours the building is locked and your ID card won't work. Weekend access in Bengaluru needs a request to the facilities team by Friday 15:00.

## 2. Office days

- Everyone works from the office **3 days a week**.
- **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are anchor days**: team rituals, design reviews and all-hands happen on these days, so your three office days should normally be these.
- Teams may agree on different days in writing with their Engineering Manager, but at least 2 of the 3 days must be anchor days.
- Your attendance is tracked through your ID card. Coming in fewer than **8 days in a calendar month** without approved leave is raised with your manager.

## 3. Booking a desk

- Desks are shared. Book one in the Kestrel Desk app **by 18:00 the day before**.
- Bookings not checked in by **11:30** are released automatically.
- Meeting rooms for more than 6 people need a booking with a purpose; rooms can't be held for more than 3 hours a day by the same person.

## 4. Working from home

- On WFH days you follow the same core hours (11:00 to 16:00 IST) and must be on Slack and reachable by phone.
- Use a secure home network. Public Wi-Fi in cafés is allowed only with the company VPN switched on.
- Customer calls must be taken from a quiet place where nobody else can hear customer details.

## 5. Working from another city

- You may work from another city in India for up to **20 working days per calendar year**, with your Engineering Manager's approval at least **5 working days** in advance.
- These days don't count as office days, so you'll be below the 3-day rule for those weeks; that's fine when approved.
- **Working from outside India is not allowed**, even for a day, because of tax and data-protection rules. Remote access from abroad is blocked automatically. Business travel abroad approved under the Travel Policy is the only exception.

## 6. Home-office support

| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Internet allowance | ₹1,000 per month, paid with your salary (no bills needed) |
| Monitor | One 24-inch monitor on request, delivered to your home |
| Chair | Reimbursed up to ₹8,000 once every 3 years, with the bill |
| Headset | One noise-cancelling headset when you join |

Interns get the internet allowance only.

Equipment stays company property. Return it on your last working day or the cost is recovered in your final settlement.

## 7. Health and safety

- Late-night office cabs are available to anyone leaving the Bengaluru office after **21:00**. Book them on the facilities desk before 19:00.
- In a medical emergency in the office, the first-aid room is on the 4th floor next to the pantry, and the front desk can call the on-site nurse between 10:00 and 19:00.

## 8. Exceptions

Medical or caregiving needs that make office days hard are handled case by case. Talk to your Engineering Manager and the HR team; temporary full-WFH can be approved for up to 3 months at a time.
