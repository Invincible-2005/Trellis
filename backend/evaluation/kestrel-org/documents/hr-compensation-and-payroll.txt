COMPENSATION AND PAYROLL GUIDE (RESTRICTED)
Owner: HR Manager | For: HR Manager only | Version: FY 2026-27, 1 April 2026

This guide holds salary bands, increment rules, bonus rules and the payroll calendar.
It is Restricted information under the Code of Conduct. Do not share it with
HR Executives, managers or employees. Managers ask the HR Manager for individual
figures; they never receive the full tables.

2. ANNUAL INCREMENT MATRIX
--------------------------

Increments are decided once a year after the April performance review and take
effect from 1 April (paid with arrears in the May salary).

Review rating   Increment range
5 (Outstanding)        12% to 15%
4 (Exceeds)             8% to 11%
3 (Meets)               5% to 7%
2 (Partly meets)        0% to 3%
1 (Below)               0%, PIP discussion

Promotion increment: a promoted person gets an extra 10% on top of their rating
increment, and at least the minimum of the new band.

The total increment budget for FY 2026-27 is 9% of the salary bill. The HR
Manager checks every team's proposals against this before letters go out.


3. ANNUAL PERFORMANCE BONUS
---------------------------

- Up to 10% of annual CTC, based on company results and the individual rating.
- Paid once a year in the June salary.
- Only people who joined before 1 October of the review year are eligible.
- People serving notice on the payout date are not eligible.


4. PAYROLL CALENDAR
-------------------

- Payroll inputs (new joiners, exits, leave without pay, allowances, approved
  expense claims) close on the 20th of every month.
- Salary is credited on the LAST WORKING DAY of the month.
- Payslips are published in the HR portal on the 1st of the next month.
- If the last working day is a bank holiday, salary is credited one working day
  earlier.
- Form 16 is issued by 15 June every year.


5. ALLOWANCES PAID THROUGH PAYROLL
----------------------------------

Allowance                     Amount
Internet (WFH)                Rs 1,000 per month (everyone, including interns)
On-call                       Rs 3,000 per week + Rs 1,500 per paged night
Night shift (rare)            Rs 500 per night
Relocation (new joiners)      Up to Rs 50,000, once, with bills

On-call claims must be approved by the Engineering Manager by the 20th to reach
that month's payroll.


6. SALARY ADVANCES AND LOANS
----------------------------

- Salary advance: up to 1 month's net salary, recovered over at most 6 months,
  only after probation. Approved by the HR Manager.
- Only one advance can be open at a time.
- No loans are given to interns or people serving notice.


7. FULL AND FINAL SETTLEMENT
----------------------------

Paid within 45 days of the last working day. It includes unpaid salary, leave
encashment of up to 15 unused earned leave days, and recovery of any unreturned
equipment or open advance.
