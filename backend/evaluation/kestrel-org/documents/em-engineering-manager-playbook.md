# Engineering Manager Playbook

**Owner:** Admin · **For:** Engineering Managers only · **Last updated:** 5 October 2026

This playbook covers the people side of running an engineering team at Kestrel: 1:1s, unblocking work, strikes, overtime, hiring and budgets. It is written for managers; please don't forward it to your team.

## 1. 1:1 meetings

- Hold a **weekly 30-minute 1:1** with each direct report at E1 to E3.
- Staff (E4) and Principal (E5) engineers may move to **fortnightly** 1:1s if both of you agree.
- Write short notes after every 1:1 in the shared notes doc. These notes feed the performance review.
- Never cancel two 1:1s in a row with the same person; reschedule instead.

## 2. Unblocking work

- When someone marks a task **Blocked** in Trellis, reply **within 4 working hours** with one of: help, a new deadline, or reassigning the task.
- If a task has been blocked for more than **2 working days**, raise it in the Thursday engineering sync.
- When you change a deadline, write the reason in the task comments so the person isn't blamed later.

## 3. Giving strikes

The Code of Conduct lists which behaviour leads to a strike. As a manager:

- **Talk to the person first.** A strike should never be the first time someone hears about a problem, except for credential sharing or data leaks.
- Write the reason in plain words, with the date and what happened. "Unprofessional" is not a reason.
- Trellis checks your reason against our policies and warns you if it can't find a matching rule; read the warning before confirming.
- When someone gets their **third strike in 12 months** it is escalated to the HR Manager. Add a note for HR with the history.
- Never give a strike for harassment complaints; those go to HR or the Internal Committee.

## 4. Overtime and weekend work

- Overtime beyond the normal 9-hour day may be approved by you up to **10 hours per week** per person.
- Anything above 10 hours a week, or overtime for more than **3 weeks in a row**, needs the **HR Manager's** approval.
- Weekend work earns **one comp-off day for each weekend day worked**. Comp-offs must be used within **60 days** or they lapse.
- Interns may not work overtime or weekends.

## 5. Expenses you approve

- You approve your team's travel requests and expense claims **up to ₹40,000 per claim**. Larger claims go to the Finance Manager automatically.
- **Team events budget:** ₹2,500 per person per quarter, for team lunches, offsites or celebrations. It doesn't carry over to the next quarter.
- Check that receipts are attached for items above ₹500 before approving.

## 6. Hiring

- New headcount needs a **headcount request approved by the Admin**. Replacements for people who resigned need only HR approval.
- Our interview loop: recruiter screen, technical round, system design (E3 and above), and a values round with you.
- You give a hire/no-hire decision **within 2 working days** of the last round.
- Offers and salary are handled by HR. Don't discuss numbers with candidates.

## 7. Low performance

- If someone is consistently below expectations, discuss it in 1:1s and write it down for at least **one month** first.
- A **performance improvement plan (PIP)** lasts **60 days** and is always started together with the HR Manager.
- During a PIP, hold weekly check-ins and record progress against the written goals.

## 8. On-call fairness

- Nobody should be on call more than **one week in four**.
- People returning from leave of more than 2 weeks skip their next rotation.
- Approve on-call allowance claims every month by the 20th so they reach payroll.

## 9. Attrition warning signs

Watch for: someone stopping speaking in team meetings, a sudden drop in code reviews, many single-day leaves, or a request for their own performance notes. Talk to the person early. If someone hands in a resignation, inform HR the **same day**.
