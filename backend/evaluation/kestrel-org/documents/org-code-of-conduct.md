# Kestrel Software: Code of Conduct

**Owner:** HR Manager · **Applies to:** everyone at Kestrel Software Pvt Ltd, including interns and contractors · **Version:** 4.0, effective 1 April 2026

Kestrel builds KestrelTrack, the delivery-tracking platform that more than 300 logistics companies rely on every day. Our customers trust us with their drivers' locations, their shipments and their business data. This code explains how we treat each other, our customers and that data. If something isn't covered here, ask your manager or the HR team on #ask-hr.

## 1. Our values

1. **Own the outcome.** If you start something, finish it or hand it over clearly.
2. **Say it early.** Bad news shared early is a problem; bad news shared late is a crisis.
3. **Respect people's time.** Be on time for meetings, keep them short and write things down.
4. **Protect the customer.** Customer data is never a shortcut, a test fixture or a demo.

## 2. Respect and harassment

Kestrel has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying, in the office, online, at offsites and at client sites.

- Sexual harassment complaints go to the **Internal Committee (IC)** under the POSH Act, at ic@kestrelsoftware.example. A complaint should be made **within 3 months** of the incident. The IC finishes its inquiry within 90 days.
- Other complaints (bullying, discrimination, unfair treatment) go to the HR Manager or through the anonymous grievance form on the intranet.
- Harassment is never handled with a strike. It always goes straight to HR or the IC, and can lead to termination.
- Nobody may be punished for raising a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a serious violation.

## 3. Working hours and attendance

- Our **core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST**. Everyone (except people on approved leave) must be reachable on Slack during core hours.
- A normal working day is 9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break. Office days and work-from-home rules are in the Hybrid Work Policy.
- If you will be unavailable during core hours, post in your team channel **before 11:00** that day.
- **Unexcused absence** means missing core hours without telling anyone. Three unexcused absences in one calendar month count as a conduct issue (see section 7).

## 4. Confidential information

We classify information in four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. The Information Security Policy defines each level.

- Never share Confidential or Restricted information outside Kestrel, including with friends who work in the same industry.
- Salary details, performance ratings and customer contracts are **Restricted**. Only the people who need them may see them.
- Don't discuss customer names or incidents in public places, on social media or in public Slack communities.
- When you leave Kestrel, your confidentiality obligations continue for **2 years**.

## 5. Accounts and passwords

- Your Kestrel login is personal. **Never share your password, MFA codes or session with anyone, not even a teammate** who "just needs to check something". If someone needs access, they request their own through the IT Helpdesk.
- Sharing credentials leads to an immediate password reset by IT and a strike.

## 6. Conflicts of interest and gifts

- Declare any conflict of interest (for example a side business, a relative working for a vendor or customer, or freelance work in logistics software) to the HR Manager **within 7 days** of it arising.
- You may accept gifts from customers or vendors worth **up to ₹2,000**. Anything above that must be declined politely or handed to HR, who will raffle it at the year-end party.
- Never accept cash, gift cards or travel from a vendor during a purchase decision you are part of.
- Outside employment needs written approval from the HR Manager.

## 7. Strikes

A strike is a formal, recorded warning given in Trellis by a manager who has permission to give strikes (normally the Engineering Manager). The person receives it in their Trellis inbox along with the reason.

**Behaviour that leads to a strike:**

| Behaviour | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sharing your password, MFA code or account | IT also resets the account |
| Missing an agreed deadline without telling anyone beforehand | Telling your lead early is never a strike |
| Three unexcused absences in a calendar month | See section 3 |
| Sharing Confidential or Restricted data outside Kestrel | Serious cases go straight to the HR Manager |
| Deploying to production outside the deploy rules without approval | See the Engineering Handbook |
| Not reporting a lost laptop or phone within the required time | See the Information Security Policy |

**What happens next:**

- Strikes count over a **rolling 12 months**.
- The **third strike within 12 months is escalated** automatically. The HR Manager reviews it within **10 working days** and may start a performance improvement plan or a disciplinary process.
- You can **appeal a strike within 7 calendar days** from the Feedback page in Trellis. The appeal goes to a reviewer who is senior to the person who gave the strike.
- Strikes are not given for honest mistakes that were reported promptly, for low performance on its own (that is handled in reviews), or for harassment (see section 2).

## 8. Social media and public speaking

- You may say you work at Kestrel. Don't speak on Kestrel's behalf without approval from the Admin team.
- Talks and blog posts about our technology must be reviewed by your Engineering Manager **2 weeks** before publication.

## 9. Reporting a violation

Report anything that breaks this code to your manager, the HR Manager or the anonymous grievance form. Reports are handled confidentially. If you're not sure whether something is a violation, report it anyway.
